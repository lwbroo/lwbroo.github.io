Overview

A personal Western astrology journal.

Name TBD is a personal Western astrology record and analysis app, designed to help you see the trail of your own chart over time. It brings your natal chart, planetary positions, daily transits, and mood-energy notes into one quiet place.

The first version is being shaped for a global English-speaking audience.

How it works

Three steps, once a day.

  1. 01

    Enter birth details

    Date, time, and place — set once.

  2. 02

    Generate natal chart

    Your natal wheel with planetary positions.

  3. 03

    Follow daily transits

    Note your state each day and review the trend.
