The craft

How it’s being designed.

Name TBD is a personal Western astrology record and analysis app, designed to help you see the trail of your own chart over time. It brings your natal chart, planetary positions, daily transits, and mood-energy notes into one quiet place. The first version is being shaped for a global English-speaking audience.

細節 03

Today, along a timeline.
