Studio

Records before noise.

Quiet software for personal patterns.

lwbro studio is a small independent studio making personal record and analysis apps. The work is intentionally focused: give one person a calm place to keep meaningful data and review how it changes.

  • Small studio, made by hand.

    Every screen is designed and built in-house, one detail at a time.

  • Local-first.

    Your records stay on your device whenever possible.

  • Records, not feeds.

    Built for long-term records and review — not social comparison or distraction.

  • Privacy by default.

    No accounts unless a feature truly needs one; your data is yours.

A note from the studio

lwbro studio is one person.

I design and build every screen myself, because the tools I use every day should feel calm, honest, and made by hand.

If something doesn’t work for you, or you have an idea — write to me directly. I read every message.
